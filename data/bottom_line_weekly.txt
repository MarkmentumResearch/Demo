Bottom Line: Macro conditions reflect slowing sequential growth with pressured earnings expectations and still-sticky-but-easing inflation, and that backdrop is echoed in the levers: a weaker USD, powerful bid to Gold and the broader energy complex, and largely unchanged yields and bonds point to the market leaning into a late-cycle, stagflation-lite mix of persistent price pressure and deteriorating growth, rewarding real assets and quality growth while penalizing more economically sensitive and rate-exposed pockets.
