Bottom Line: Macro conditions reflect a backdrop where growth is slowing and earnings revisions remain under pressure even as inflation, while easing from prior peaks, stays above pre-COVID norms, and the combination of surging Gold (+15.6%), a softer USD (-0.8%), higher yields (+1.6%), positive bond returns (+0.5%), and a strong energy complex (+10.4%) signals markets are repositioning for a stickier-inflation, late-cycle environment rather than a clean, disinflationary re-acceleration in growth.
